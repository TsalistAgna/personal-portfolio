A clearer path from a first rental to a growing business.

Project Overview

Grow Academy is a landing-page concept for a fictional short-term rental education platform. I brought two distinct learning paths into one coherent experience: Launch for beginners with zero or one rental unit, and Scale for existing operators who need clearer systems, team processes, and room to grow.

My focus was to help visitors identify their stage, understand the programmes, and choose an appropriate next step.

This portfolio version adapts an earlier design exercise using fictional branding and revised content.

The Design Challenge

Two starting points. One clear experience.

Beginners need a clear starting point, while experienced operators need to understand how the programme supports more structured operations. I needed to address both audiences without making visitors work through irrelevant information.

How might a landing page help visitors recognise their stage, understand the right programme, and confidently choose their next step?

Experience Design

Help visitors find their starting point.

I structured the intended journey around four steps: recognise your stage → explore the programme → understand the learning path → choose a next step.

04

Choose a next step

Find a relevant entry point after understanding the offer.

I designed two primary calls to action: register for an introductory webinar for beginners, and book a strategy call for existing operators. These are proposed actions within the landing-page concept, not functioning services.

Interface Direction

I used indigo and lavender to create visual continuity between the introduction, programme sections, and next steps. Clear typography and generous whitespace establish a reading order and make the long page easier to scan.

Modular programme cards put comparable information in a consistent format. Alternating light and dark sections separate topics, helping visitors recognise when the page moves from learning foundations to operations and growth.

The dashboard-style illustration in the cover is a curriculum preview, not a functioning product. I used it to make the learning concept tangible by showing how topics could connect—from a first unit to everyday operations.

Full Landing Page

The first part introduces the offer and the Launch and Scale programmes. The second brings together toolkits, programme roadmaps, an illustrative learning scenario, FAQs, and next steps.

Key Design Decisions

  • Stage-based navigation: I organised the page around Launch and Scale to help visitors recognise which path fits their current situation.
  • Distinct calls to action: I gave each audience a relevant entry point, making the next step specific to their needs.
  • Programme roadmaps: I used clear, sequential steps to make the proposed progression easier to understand.
  • Practical resources: I used toolkit cards and a labelled learning scenario to explain the course concept without relying on unverified social proof.
  • A structured long page: I repeated relevant actions at key points and used visual hierarchy to guide visitors through detailed information.

Reflection & Next Steps

The result is a completed visual concept that connects two learning paths within one landing page. It presents my approach to information hierarchy and programme comparison; its effectiveness still needs to be evaluated with visitors.

My proposed next steps are to:

  • Test whether visitors can distinguish Launch from Scale.
  • Check whether the programme content answers their main questions.
  • Explore responsive layouts for smaller screens.
  • Review contrast, text sizes, and CTA clarity.
  • Refine the content based on feedback.

My Role
