Bloom

Project Overview

Bloom is a responsive postpartum mental well-being website concept for mothers in Indonesia. It brings together gentle emotional check-ins, a screening experience, progress tracking, calming activities, and ways to reach out for support in one welcoming space.

The concept uses the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS) as a future screening framework, but screening is not a diagnosis. The designed prototype presents a clearly labeled demonstration experience while a real clinical implementation would require a validated questionnaire, reviewed scoring, and appropriate referral and privacy safeguards.

I shaped the product direction, planned the core journeys, designed the screens in Figma with AI-assisted tools, and developed and iterated on the web prototype using Berrry.app.

The Problem

Emotional support should not end with a screening result

After childbirth, mothers may experience exhaustion, unfamiliar emotional changes, or difficulty asking for help. A single questionnaire can raise awareness, but it does not automatically explain the next step or make everyday support easier to access.

The question behind Bloom was: how might a digital experience help a mother recognize how she feels, reflect on changes over time, and move from awareness toward practical and professional support without making her feel judged or diagnosed?

From Idea to Scope

I developed Bloom around a connected journey rather than a one-time test: begin with a gentle check-in, revisit patterns, choose a supportive action, and know where to find human care. For the hackathon, I prioritized a coherent web experience with a manageable set of meaningful features.

Interface Direction

The desktop screens combine a lavender hero, small flower icons, rounded feature cards, and a clear navigation system. The screening page intentionally feels like part of the same supportive experience rather than a separate clinical form.

My Journey, Calm Corner, and Support extend the same visual language with a rounded-bar feelings chart, interactive breathing concept, and a clearly separated urgent-help panel.

Responsive Experience

Mobile is not simply a smaller desktop layout. The welcome screen gives the mascot room to introduce Bloom, while subsequent pages prioritize one task at a time and use five persistent navigation destinations: Home, Check, Journal, Calm, and Help.

Key Features & Product Decisions

  • Screening without diagnosis: Design the EPDS-inspired pathway to provide context and next steps, not a fixed medical verdict. The prototype uses a demonstration questionnaire rather than claiming clinical validation.
  • Reflection over time: Separate daily mood check-ins from formal screening history, so each record has a clear meaning and mothers can review previous entries.
  • Low-effort calming tools: Bring breathing, grounding, fidget interactions, and gentle audio concepts into one Calm Corner that can be used whenever someone wants a pause.
  • Bloom Buddy: Explore a focused AI conversation companion for postpartum feelings and user-selected needs, with boundaries against diagnosis and an easy route to human support.
  • Support as an action: Make it easier to prepare for a professional conversation or ask a trusted person for practical help, instead of ending the journey at a score.
  • Careful data handling: Treat mood entries and screening responses as sensitive information. Persistent accounts, secure histories, consent, and deletion are important implementation considerations rather than assumed guarantees.
  • Indonesia-first approach: Design for Bahasa Indonesia and English, familiar healthcare pathways, and culturally appropriate, nonjudgmental language.

Prototype & Next Steps

The live website is an evolving AI-assisted prototype. It demonstrates Bloom's connected design direction, while clinical screening validation, secure personal-data handling, and the reliability of all live integrations need separate testing before the product could be used as a real healthcare service.

The next priorities are reliable saved screening history, clearer distinction between mood data and screening results, end-to-end testing across accounts and devices, and careful safety review of Bloom Buddy and help-seeking flows.
